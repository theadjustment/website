1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit a website. They allow websites to remember your device, preferences, and session state, enabling secure navigation and tailored features.

In addition to HTTP cookies, web browsers utilize browser local storage (LocalStorage), which retains settings on your device without automatically sending them to the server on every page request.

2. How The Adjustment Point Uses Cookies

We follow the principle of Privacy by Default and strict compliance with the Google Consent Mode v2 framework:

  • Non-essential cookies (Google Analytics and Google Maps) are actively blocked when you land on the site.
  • No tracking scripts run and no external map assets load until you explicitly click "Accept All" or toggle specific categories in our preferences center.
  • You have the permanent right to revoke or adjust your choices at any moment with one click.

4. External Services & BookUp Appointments

Online appointments are handled via the external platform BookUp (bookup.gr). While browsing adjustmentpoint.gr, no cookies are placed by BookUp.

When you click "Book an Appointment", you are directed to www.bookup.gr/theadjustmentpoint, which operates under its own Privacy and Cookie policies.

5. How to Manage Cookies in Your Browser

In addition to our on-site settings modal, you can control or delete cookies through your web browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies (Chrome Guide)
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data (Firefox Guide)
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies (Safari Guide)
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies (Edge Guide)
